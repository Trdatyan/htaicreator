HT-AIcreator

A Windows desktop app that helps creators turn their own long videos into short vertical clips.

What it does

  • Transcribes speech on your computer.
  • Uses an AI model to suggest the most engaging moments and titles.
  • Crops to 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1 with face tracking, and adds optional subtitles.
  • Lets you review the clips and publish the ones you choose to your own TikTok account.
