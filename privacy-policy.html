HT-AIcreator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

HT-AIcreator ("the App") is a desktop application for Windows that helps a user cut long videos into short clips, add subtitles, and, optionally, publish those clips to the user's own TikTok account. The App is operated by Hrant Trdatyan ("we", "us"). Contact: globus.company18@mail.ru.

1. Summary

The App runs on your computer. We do not operate a server that receives your videos, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data. The only data that leaves your computer is sent to the services you choose to connect, as described below.

2. Data processed on your computer

  • Videos you select. Video files are read locally. Speech is converted to text locally using an open-source speech recognition model. Clips, subtitle files and text transcripts are saved in a folder next to the original video.
  • Settings. Your preferences (for example subtitle style) are stored in a settings file in your user folder on your computer.
  • Access keys and tokens. If you enter an AI service key or a TikTok access token, it is stored in the same local settings file on your computer, and is used only to call the corresponding service on your behalf. You can delete it at any time by removing the file or clearing the field.

3. Data sent to third-party services

  • AI service (Anthropic API). To find the most interesting moments and suggest titles, the App sends the text transcript of your video (not the video or audio itself) to the Anthropic API using the key you provide. Anthropic's handling of this data is governed by Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy.
  • TikTok. Only if you choose to connect your TikTok account and start publishing, the App sends the selected video clip, its title and the privacy setting you choose to TikTok through the TikTok Content Posting API. The App accesses only the TikTok account you authorize and only to publish content you selected. TikTok's handling of this data is governed by TikTok's terms and privacy policy.

The App does not send data to any other party and contains no advertising or analytics.

4. Data we receive

We do not receive your videos, transcripts, keys, tokens or TikTok account data. They stay on your computer or go directly from your computer to the services above.

5. Retention and deletion

Files created by the App remain on your computer until you delete them. To remove the App's stored settings and tokens, delete the settings file in your user folder, or clear the fields in the App. You can revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time in your TikTok account settings under connected apps.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your region).

7. Security

Because data is stored locally, its security depends on the security of your computer. Do not share your settings file, because it may contain your access keys.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
